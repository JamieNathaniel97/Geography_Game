Games

Find: a name, capital or flag appears at the top. Tap that country on the map.

Name: tap a country, then type its name, capital or flag's country. Small spelling slips are forgiven.

Pick the game and the region, then Start. Give up shows the answers; tap any country to review them.

Learning mode

No score: tap anything to read its card. More facts opens the rest, and Focus on opens a country's own map of states, provinces or counties.

The map

Drag to move, pinch or scroll to zoom. Tiny countries have a circle round them. The buttons on the right switch Borders, Terrain and globe or flat map.

Countries

The Countries tab has games on one country's own map: US states, Canada's provinces, British and Irish counties, and more.

Options and stats

The cog sets a timer, Knowledge gaps (the countries you most need to practise) and the light or dark theme. The chart button at the top of the map shows your stats and best times.
